PageMind 隐私政策

最后更新日期：2026 年 5 月 10 日

PageMind 是一个浏览器扩展程序，帮助用户总结网页内容、 基于当前网页进行问答，并使用 AI 分析网页信息。本隐私政策说明 PageMind 会处理哪些信息， 以及这些信息将如何被使用。

1. 我们处理的信息

当你主动使用 PageMind 对网页进行总结、提问或分析时，扩展程序可能会处理以下信息：

  • 你请求分析的当前网页内容。
  • 当前网页的标题和 URL。
  • 你在 PageMind 中输入的问题、提示词或操作指令。
  • 为提供服务所需的基础技术信息，例如请求时间和错误日志。

2. 信息的使用方式

PageMind 处理这些信息仅用于提供扩展程序的核心功能，包括：

  • 生成网页内容摘要。
  • 基于网页内容回答用户问题。
  • 分析网页文本、图片或其他用户主动请求处理的内容。
  • 维护服务稳定性、排查错误并改进基础功能。

3. 数据传输

为了提供 AI 功能，PageMind 可能会将用户主动请求分析的网页内容和用户输入发送至 PageMind 后端服务：

https://api.openclaw-deploy.top

后端服务会处理相关内容，并将 AI 生成的结果返回给扩展程序。

4. 数据共享

PageMind 不出售用户数据，不会将用户数据用于广告投放、行为追踪、信用评估或与功能无关的第三方营销。

为提供用户主动请求的 AI 功能，部分内容可能会在必要时由第三方 AI 服务提供商处理。

5. 数据存储

PageMind 可能会在浏览器本地存储有限的设置信息，例如扩展偏好设置或临时状态。

我们不会主动收集敏感个人信息。用户应避免通过扩展程序提交密码、私密凭据、金融信息、 医疗信息或其他敏感数据。

6. 用户控制

PageMind 仅在用户主动使用扩展程序时处理网页内容。用户可以随时停止使用 PageMind， 或从浏览器中卸载该扩展程序。

7. 安全措施

PageMind 使用 HTTPS 与后端服务通信。我们会采取合理措施保护传输数据，并防止未经授权的访问。

8. 政策更新

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。任何变更都会发布在本页面，并更新日期。

PageMind Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 10, 2026

PageMind is a browser extension that helps users summarize web pages, ask questions based on the current page, and analyze page content with AI. This Privacy Policy explains what information PageMind processes and how it is used.

1. Information We Process

When you actively use PageMind to summarize, ask questions, or analyze a webpage, the extension may process the following information:

  • The content of the current webpage selected or accessed for analysis.
  • The title and URL of the current webpage.
  • The questions, prompts, or instructions you enter into PageMind.
  • Basic technical information required to provide the service, such as request time and error logs.

2. How We Use Information

The information processed by PageMind is used only to provide the core functionality of the extension, including:

  • Generating summaries of webpage content.
  • Answering user questions based on webpage content.
  • Analyzing webpage text, images, or other user-requested content.
  • Improving stability, debugging errors, and maintaining the service.

3. Data Transmission

To provide AI-powered features, PageMind may send user-requested webpage content and user prompts to the PageMind backend service:

https://api.openclaw-deploy.top

The backend service processes the content and returns the AI-generated result to the extension.

4. Data Sharing

PageMind does not sell user data. PageMind does not use user data for advertising, behavioral tracking, credit evaluation, or unrelated third-party marketing.

Some content may be processed by third-party AI service providers only when necessary to provide the user-requested AI functionality.

5. Data Storage

PageMind may store limited local settings in the browser, such as extension preferences or temporary state required for functionality.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information. Users should avoid submitting passwords, private credentials, financial information, medical information, or other sensitive data through the extension.

6. User Control

PageMind only processes webpage content when the user actively uses the extension. Users can stop using PageMind at any time or uninstall the extension from the browser.

7. Security

PageMind uses HTTPS for communication with its backend service. We take reasonable measures to protect transmitted data and prevent unauthorized access.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
